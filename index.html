How Mooncast decides

The score combines trend alignment, breakout structure, relative volume, RSI, MACD and volatility. BUY requires a high score; WAIT means confirmation is incomplete; AVOID means conditions are weak or volatility is unsuitable. The same market can produce different setups on different timeframes.

⚠️ This is a research and paper-trading tool. A BUY signal is not a guarantee of profit. Backtest and paper-test before risking real money.
